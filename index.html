Featured Research Project

LotusAI: Clinical Recommendation Engine

Built for a USF research initiative, LotusAI is an AI-assisted recommendation system designed to support clinically aligned meal planning in a research setting.

The platform was designed as a modular decision-support workflow that helps translate dietary guidance into personalized recommendations while maintaining traceability and research alignment.

  • Problem: Researchers needed a more scalable way to turn clinical guidance into personalized meal recommendations.
  • Approach: Built a modular AI-supported workflow over vetted research inputs, designed for consistency, traceability, and future evaluation.
  • Outcome: Created an auditable recommendation process suitable for research validation and future productization.
